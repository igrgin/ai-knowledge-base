## Authentication amendment for design

Full-project sharing and authorized importing are basic permissions for ordinary users and administrators, individually revocable. Consume current T3 permission checks before each new share/import admission alongside ownership, recipient and quota checks. Preserve the already-admitted-work versus fresh-request contract; administrator status does not grant access to another account's private source project.

Consume T3's stable application account identity for owner and recipient authorization. The Google provider choice does not redefine named invitations as email-text matching or registered-link imports as anonymous link possession. Agree named-recipient identity/proof before implementation; preserve accepted independent-copy and registered-link semantics. Account email changes must not transfer projects or existing grants. A validated Google email claim alone does not universally prove present control of an external mailbox; resolve that distinction without silently restricting login to Gmail/Workspace or adding an email service. Reuse T3's identity interface. The developer shortcut is not a separate sharing mode or authorization for real users' resources.
